      How it's worked out

      Hours a year = (minutes each time × times a year × people + times a year × share that goes wrong × minutes to fix × people) ÷ 60

      Times a year: per working day × 250, per week × 52, per month × 12, per year × 1.

      Cost a year = hours a year × hourly cost
